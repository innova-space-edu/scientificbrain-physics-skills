# Skill card: workflow-skill-creator

- Status: v0.3.0
- Purpose: convert a validated completed workflow into a draft reusable physics skill.
- Inputs: workflow artifacts, decisions, dependencies, validation evidence, failures and regime limits.
- Outputs: draft skill scaffold, dependency map, guardrails and three-layer eval plan.
- Helper: scripts/workflow_skill_scaffold.py.
- Non-goal: automatic self-modification or promotion to stable.
- Inspiration: reusable workflow/skill patterns described by Google DeepMind Science Skills, reimplemented for ScientificBrain.
